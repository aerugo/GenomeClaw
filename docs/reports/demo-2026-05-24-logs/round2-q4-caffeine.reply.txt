**Practical recommendation:**  
If your target bedtime is ~11 PM, stop coffee/caffeine by **3 PM**. If you’re prone to insomnia, anxiety, or waking overnight, make it **noon** for two weeks and track sleep latency + awakenings.

Why: controlled trials show that **400 mg caffeine** can disrupt sleep even **6 hours before bedtime**, and newer dose/timing work suggests lower doses are less disruptive while larger doses need a longer runway. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24235903/?utm_source=openai))

So I’d frame this as: **you don’t have an obvious genetic “bad caffeine handler” signal, but your best cutoff should be phenotype-tested.** Try:
1. **2 weeks:** caffeine only before noon, max 100–200 mg/day.  
2. If sleep doesn’t improve, you can probably tolerate later coffee.  
3. If sleep improves, keep the earlier cutoff.
