**Practical takeaway:** don’t change any medication based on this. But if you’re ever prescribed clopidogrel, warfarin, codeine/tramadol, high-dose simvastatin, thiopurines, fluoropyrimidines, abacavir, allopurinol, or carbamazepine/oxcarbazepine, it would be reasonable to ask the prescriber/pharmacist whether a clinical PGx test is appropriate.
